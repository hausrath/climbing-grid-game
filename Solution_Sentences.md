Solution Sentences

**Hand choice only**

* Start off on the correct hand or you'll be forced into crosses you can't recover from
* Start off on the correct hand where there is more than one choice (branch at start) to avoid crosses you can't recover from
* Grab a bad crimp up front, instead of a better hold, to set up for the correct hand later on

**Hand choice, shake**

* Shake out so you don't fall off the wall
* Shake after the cross to make it to the top



**Hand choice, chalk**

* Chalk so that you don't fall off the wall
* Chalk before the crimp section, even if you don't need it as much, so you can make it through the crux



**Hand choice, cross**

* Use the cross skill to avoid penalties and make it to the top
