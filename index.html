How to Play
Goal:
Climb as high as you can! Game ends when pump reaches max or grip reaches 0. Increase your max with stats!
Progression:
• Gain +10 XP per successful hold grab
• Bonus +25 XP every 5 holds
• Level up when XP bar fills
• XP requirements scale 1.5x per level
Stats (3 pts per level):
• Endurance: -2% pump, +5 max pump
• Power: -2% grip loss, +5 max grip
• Speed: Reduces cooldowns
• Technique: +10% movement bonuses
Mechanics:
• Hold Difficulty → Grip costs
• Move Difficulty → Pump costs
• Rest holds (⚓) = no cooldowns
• Long/diagonal moves cost more pump
Hand Selection:
• Press A = Left Hand
• Press D = Right Hand
• Must alternate hands: L→R→L or R→L→R
• Match on ★ holds to reset everything
Climbing:
• Only holds in the GOLD HIGHLIGHTED row are climbable
• Upper holds are visible for planning ahead
• Select hand + movement style, then click hold
Movement Style:
• Press 1 = Dynamic (far: +10% success)
• Press 2 = Regular (balanced, no cooldown)
• Press 3 = Static (close: +10% success)
• Dynamic & Static have 3-move cooldown!

Static: +10% success on close moves
Dynamic: +10% success on far moves
Regular: No bonus, no cooldown
Actions:
• Press Q = Shake Out (-20 pump)
• Press E = Chalk Up (+20 grip)
• Both have 5-move cooldown
• Use strategically to extend your climb!
Combo System:
• 3 consecutive successful grabs = Flow State!
• Flow State: ALL moves cost 50% pump & grip
• Flow State persists as long as you keep succeeding
• Failed grabs break combo and reset flow state
Cross-over Rules:
• Only SUCCESSFUL grabs count toward crosses
• 1st successful cross: FREE (no penalty)
• All consecutive crosses after: +3 pump each
• Natural reach or match resets count
Strategy:
• Look ahead - plan sequence
• Use Static for close moves
• Use Dynamic for far holds
• Match (★) resets hands & crosses
